Case Study

Non-Contact Cage & Pooter

Mechatronics Engineer · BigSis (Zyzzle Ltd), UK
Problem

Exposed mechanical handling caused product damage and generated debris that blocked the downstream vision system.

Approach
  • Engineered an induced-air pooter for gentle, non-contact product handling
  • Designed a protective cage architecture to prevent product escape and exposure to moving parts
  • Optimised airflow parameters by calculation for controlled, safe, and efficient return transport
Result

Eliminated contact-related product damage and vision-system blockages while improving handling reliability and system cleanliness.
