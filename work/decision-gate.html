Case Study

Intelligent Decision-Gate Routing

Lead Mechatronics Engineer · BigSis (Zyzzle Ltd), UK
Problem

Product routing logic relied on an over-complex separation mechanism, hurting maintainability and uptime.

Approach
  • Engineered a simplified separation mechanism with integrated mechanical gating logic for intelligent routing
  • Prototyped a further 3-way decision-gate mechanism to extend routing capability
Result

Reduced part count and system complexity while improving maintainability, separation accuracy, and operational uptime.
