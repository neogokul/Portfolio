Case Study

Fail-Safe Distribution

Mechatronics Engineer · BigSis (Zyzzle Ltd), UK
Problem

Single-channel product flow was vulnerable to full-line stoppage from a single blockage.

Approach
  • Redesigned flow by splitting single-channel systems into multiple parallel channels
  • Engineered a fail-safe distribution architecture that keeps product moving through partial blockages
Result

Adopted across several production systems, the design keeps product flowing uninterrupted despite partial blockages.
