Cold-Tunnel Hibernation Sorter

Lead Mechatronics Engineer · BigSis (Zyzzle Ltd), UK

Drop image at

Drop image at

As-builtRev C
Problem

Downstream handling and quality checks required product to be temporarily and safely hibernated via controlled cooling, without breaching biosecurity or uniformity requirements.

Approach
  • Engineered a multi-fan forced-convection cooling system for uniform airflow and thermal gradients across the tunnel
  • Integrated a precision wiper mechanism to distribute product evenly for consistent thermal exposure
  • Designed a converging guide to feed the cooling channel, and a feedscrew-driven transport to regulate residence time
Result

Consistent hibernation duration and thermal exposure across every unit, with mechanical sealing preventing humid-air ingress — scaled cleanly into full production.

Problem

Downstream processing needed capsule inversion and product separation, but separate actuation stages added cost and mechanical complexity.

Approach
  • Defined a precision tolerancing strategy for synchronised inversion and separation
  • Developed a feedscrew system that performs both inversion and separation from a single actuator
  • Engineered a tight-tolerance separator for reliable product extraction, with feedscrew-driven transport for uniform feeding
Result

Actuator consolidation reduced mechanical complexity while improving extraction consistency and overall line stability.

Problem

Bulk product formed crystal-like structuring that prevented reliable entry into individual flow channels, causing repeated entry failures.

Approach
  • Iterated multiple prototypes to identify and address the bulk-instability failure mode
  • Engineered a non-contact mechanism that disrupts internal bulk bonding without mechanically disturbing individual products
  • Divided bulk flow into controlled individual lines feeding downstream channels
Result

Eliminated entry failures from bulk crystal formation and stabilised flow consistency without compromising product integrity.
